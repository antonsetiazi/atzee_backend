🎯 DEFINISI VERTICAL: DISTRIBUTOR
=========================================
    Aplikasi untuk perusahaan yang:
        - Membeli barang dari Principal / Supplier
        - Menyimpan di Gudang
        - Menjual ke Toko / Retail / Reseller
        - Mengelola Sales Lapangan
        - Mengatur Piutang
        - Mengatur Wilayah distribusi
        - Mengelola retur & klaim

🧱 ENTITY KHUSUS DISTRIBUTOR
=========================================
    Berikut entity tambahan yang masuk verticals/distributor/

    1. Principal
        Principal = Brand Owner / Produsen

        Field tambahan:
            - exclusive_region
            - contract_start
            - contract_end
            - credit_terms
            - rebate_scheme

        Relasi:
            - Principal → Products
            - Principal → Purchase Contracts

    2. Salesman / Sales Team
        Field:
            - assigned_region
            - target_monthly
            - commission_scheme
            - supervisor

        Relasi:
            - Salesman → Customer
            - Salesman → Sales Order


    3. Distribution Area / Territory
        Field:
            - region_name
            - coverage_area
            - manager
            - active

        Relasi:
            - Territory → Salesman
            - Territory → Customer


    4. Delivery Order (DO)
        Flow:
            Sales Order → Delivery Order → Invoice → Payment

        Field tambahan:
            - vehicle
            - driver
            - dispatch_time
            - delivered_time
            - proof_of_delivery


    5. Sales Return & Purchase Return
        Fitur:
            - Return reason
            - Damage category
            - Claim to principal
            - Auto accounting impact
            - Auto stock movement


🔁 WORKFLOW UTAMA DISTRIBUTOR
=========================================
    1. Purchase Flow
        Purchase Order
            ↓
        Goods Receipt
            ↓
        Stock Update
            ↓
        Supplier Invoice
            ↓
        Payment

    2. Sales Flow (Field Sales)
        Salesman Create Sales Order
            ↓
        Warehouse Generate Delivery Order
            ↓
        Stock Deduction
            ↓
        Invoice
            ↓
        Payment / Credit
            ↓
        Aging Monitoring

    3. Return Flow
        Customer Return
            ↓
        Warehouse Inspection
            ↓
        Stock Adjustment
            ↓
        Credit Note
