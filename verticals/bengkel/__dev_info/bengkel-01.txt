🔧 VERTICAL: BENGKEL (WORKSHOP APP)
=========================================
    Vertical ini ditujukan untuk:
        - Bengkel motor
        - Bengkel mobil
        - Service AC mobil
        - Body repair
        - Tune-up specialist
        - UMKM perbengkelan


🎯 TUJUAN VERTICAL BENGKEL
=========================================
    Vertical Bengkel bertugas menangani:
        - Job service kendaraan
        - Tracking kendaraan customer
        - Sparepart & inventory
        - Mekanik assignment
        - Estimasi & invoice
        - Riwayat service

    Dan SEMUA itu harus berdiri di atas:
        - Core (auth, tenant, permission)
        - Business (transaction, inventory, accounting engine)

    Vertical hanya orchestration.


🏗 ARSITEKTUR DI ATZEE
=========================================
    core/
    business/
    verticals/
        bengkel/


🧩 ENTITY YANG DIBUTUHKAN
=========================================
    1. Customer Vehicle
        Karena bengkel unik: customer punya kendaraan.

        Entity:
            Vehicle
                - customer
                - plate_number
                - brand
                - model
                - year
                - engine_number
                - chassis_number
                - transmission_type
                - notes

        👉 Business layer hanya tahu “Partner”
        👉 Vertical menambahkan “Vehicle”


    2. Service Job (Work Order)
        Ini adalah jantung bengkel.

        WorkOrder
            - code
            - vehicle
            - odometer
            - complaint
            - diagnosis
            - status
            - priority
            - estimated_finish
            - assigned_mechanic
            - checklist_json
            - notes

        Status lifecycle:
            draft
            inspection
            waiting_approval
            in_progress
            waiting_parts
            done
            invoiced
            closed
            cancelled


    3. Service Item (Jasa)
        Berbeda dengan sparepart.

        Ini adalah jasa:
            - Tune up
            - Ganti oli
            - Balancing
            - Engine overhaul

        Entity:
            ServiceProduct

            Berdiri di atas Business Product
            Tapi type = service


    4. Sparepart Usage
        Karena bengkel sangat tergantung stok.
        Kita tidak buat inventory sendiri.
        Kita gunakan:
            business.inventory
            business.transaction

        Vertical hanya membuat:
            - WorkOrderLine
            - work_order
            - product
            - qty
            - price
            - line_type (service / part)

        Saat work order close:
            → Generate Business Transaction (sales)
            → Affect inventory
            → Affect accounting


    5. Mechanic Management
        Bisa pakai:
            business.hr (optional)

        Atau minimal:
            MechanicProfile
                - user
                - skill_level
                - specialization


    6. Appointment Booking
        Optional tapi sangat powerful untuk SaaS.

        Appointment
            - customer
            - vehicle
            - date
            - service_type
            - status


💰 FLOW KEUANGAN
=========================================
    WorkOrder → Invoice → Payment

    Tidak ada logic accounting di vertical.
    Vertical hanya panggil:
        business.transaction engine
        business.accounting engine


📊 DASHBOARD YANG DIBUTUHKAN
=========================================
