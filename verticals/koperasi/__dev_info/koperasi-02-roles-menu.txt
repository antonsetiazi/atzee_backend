1. KETUA (Full Governance)
=========================================
    Kontrol penuh terhadap koperasi

    Menu Akses
        📊 Dashboard (Full Analytics)

        👥 Members
            - List & Detail
            - Approve Member
            - Nonaktifkan Member

        💰 Savings
            - View Semua Simpanan
            - Koreksi Transaksi

        🏦 Loans
            - Approval Pinjaman
            - Override Bunga
            - Restrukturisasi

        📈 SHU
            - Generate Periode SHU
            - Approve Distribusi

        🗳 RAT
            - Buat Periode RAT
            - Finalisasi Laporan

        📑 Reports (Full)
        
        ⚙ Settings
            - Konfigurasi bunga
            - Konfigurasi simpanan wajib
            - Konfigurasi pembagian SHU

    Ketua bisa lihat semuanya.
