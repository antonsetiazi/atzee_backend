FITUR WAJIB VERTICAL POS
=========================================
    Kita bagi menjadi 5 domain besar.

    1. Master POS Configuration
        Digunakan untuk setup toko retail.

        Fitur:
            - Outlet / Store
            - Register / Cash Drawer
            - Shift Management
            - Default tax profile
            - Default price list
            - Receipt template
            - Printer setup
            - Payment method allowed

        POS tidak buat produk baru.
        POS hanya memilih price list & tax profile.


    2. Sales Transaction Engine (Real-Time)
        Ini jantung POS.

        Flow:
            - Scan / search product
            - Add to cart
            - Apply discount
            - Apply tax
            - Select payment
            - Confirm
            - Print receipt
            - Post accounting entry
            - Deduct stock

        Fitur:
            - Barcode scanning
            - Quick add product
            - Quantity control
            - Line discount
            - Cart discount
            - Multi-payment
            - Split bill
            - Hold transaction
            - Refund
            - Void transaction

        Semua posting tetap lewat:
            - Business Inventory Engine
            - Business Accounting Engine
            - Business Payment Engine


    3. Shift & Cash Control
        Retail butuh kontrol kas.

        Fitur:
            - Open shift
            - Initial cash
            - Close shift
            - Cash counting
            - Over/short detection
            - Cash movement (in/out)
            - Shift report

        Ini penting untuk audit dan fraud prevention.


    4. Reporting (Retail Focused)
        Berbeda dengan Accounting report.

        POS butuh:
            - Daily sales
            - Sales per cashier
            - Sales per outlet
            - Top product
            - Hourly sales
            - Payment method breakdown
            - Refund report

        Ini hanya agregasi dari transaksi.


    5. Offline Mode (Opsional Tapi Strategis)
        Kalau mau naik kelas:
            - Local transaction queue
            - Sync when online
            - Conflict resolver
