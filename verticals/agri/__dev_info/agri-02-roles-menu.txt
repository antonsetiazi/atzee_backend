🧭 GLOBAL MENU STRUCTURE (AGRI)
=========================================
    Ini adalah keseluruhan menu yang akan diregister oleh vertical.

    1. Dashboard
        - Overview
        - Farm Performance
        - Harvest Projection
        - Cost Summary

    2. Farm Management
        - Farms
        - Plots
        - Soil & Irrigation

    3. Crop Management
        - Crops
        - Varieties
        - Planting Templates

    4. Planting Cycles
        - Active Cycles
        - Activity Logs
        - Harvest Records

    5. Operations
        - Field Activities
        - Pest & Incident Reports
        - Weather Logs

    5. Resources
        - Workers
        - Equipment
        - Maintenance
        - Work Logs

    7. Inventory
        - Warehouses
        - Fertilizers
        - Seeds
        - Pesticides
        - Stock Movements

    8. Finance
        - Farm Expenses
        - Cost per Plot
        - Revenue per Harvest
        - Profit & ROI

    9. Reports
        - Production Reports
        - Cost Reports
        - Worker Productivity
        - Equipment Usage


Sekarang kita breakdown berdasarkan ROLE.

👑 1. OWNER (Full Visibility)
=========================================
    Persona: Pemilik lahan / perusahaan agribisnis

    Menu yang Bisa Diakses:
        ✅ Dashboard (Full)
        ✅ Farm Management (Full)
        ✅ Crop Management (Full)
        ✅ Planting Cycles (Full)
        ✅ Operations (View)
        ✅ Resources (Full)
        ✅ Inventory (Full)
        ✅ Finance (Full)
        ✅ Reports (Full)

    Owner adalah strategic-level.
    Ia tidak perlu input aktivitas harian, tapi harus melihat:
        - ROI per hektar
        - Perbandingan musim
        - Efisiensi pekerja
        - Total biaya vs hasil panen


🚜 2. FARM MANAGER
=========================================
    Persona: Pengelola operasional harian

    Menu:
        ✅ Dashboard (Operasional)
        ✅ Farm Management (View/Edit)
        ✅ Crop Management (View)
        ✅ Planting Cycles (Full)
        ✅ Operations (Full)
        ✅ Resources (Full)
        ✅ Inventory (Full)
        ⚠️ Finance (View Cost Only)
        ✅ Reports (Operasional)

    Dia bertanggung jawab:
        - Memulai siklus tanam
        - Assign worker
        - Atur pupuk
        - Monitor panen

    Tapi tidak bisa melihat laba bersih perusahaan secara penuh.


👨‍🌾 3. FIELD SUPERVISOR
=========================================
    Persona: Pengawas lapangan

    Menu:
        ✅ Dashboard (Field Overview)
        ✅ Planting Cycles (View Active Only)
        ✅ Operations (Input & Update)
        ✅ Work Logs

    Dia fokus pada:
        - Monitoring aktivitas
        - Catat hama
        - Validasi pekerjaan
        - Input laporan lapangan


👷 4. WORKER
=========================================
    Persona: Pekerja lapangan

    Menu:
        ✅ My Tasks
        ✅ Input Work Log
        ✅ Submit Activity

    Worker hanya boleh:
        - Lihat tugas hari ini
        - Input aktivitas
        - Upload foto hasil kerja
        - Submit laporan

    UI sangat minimal.


💰 5. FINANCE
=========================================
    Persona: Admin keuangan pertanian

    Menu:
        ✅ Dashboard (Cost & Revenue)
        ✅ Planting Cycles (View)
        ✅ Inventory (Stock Value View)
        ✅ Finance (Full)
        ✅ Reports (Financial Only)

    Fokus:
        - Catat biaya
        - Lihat revenue panen
        - Hitung margin
        - Analisis biaya per hektar
