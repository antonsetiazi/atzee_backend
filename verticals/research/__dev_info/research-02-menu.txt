🧭 STRUKTUR MENU GLOBAL – Research Management System

Ini adalah seluruh kemungkinan menu dalam verticals.research.

    1. Dashboard
    =========================================
        - Overview
        - My Tasks
        - Pending Approvals
        - Deadlines & Milestones

    2. Governance
    =========================================
        - Research Programs
        - Divisions / Departments
        - Reviewer Pool
        - Committees
        - Policies
        - Ethics Review

    3. Research Pipeline
    =========================================
        - Research Ideas
        - Proposals
        - Proposal Reviews
        - Approved Projects
        - Rejected / Archived

    4. Active Projects
    =========================================
        - Project List
        - Milestones
        - Experiment Logs
        - Team Management
        - Project Documents
        - Risk Log

    5. Funding & Budget
    =========================================
        - Funding Sources
        - Grants
        - Budget Plan
        - Budget Allocation
        - Expense Records
        - Budget Realization

    6. Publications & Outputs
    =========================================
        - Publications
        - Patents
        - Prototypes
        - Software Output
        - Dataset Registry

    7. Reports
    =========================================
        - Research Activity Report
        - Budget Report
        - Performance Report
        - Publication Report

    8. Archive
    =========================================
        - Completed Projects
        - Archived Proposals
        - Research Repository


Sekarang kita mapping berdasarkan role.

    1. Research Director
    =========================================
        Fokus: kontrol penuh vertical

        Menu yang terlihat:
            - Dashboard (Global)
            - Governance (Full Access)
            - Research Pipeline (Full)
            - Active Projects (All Projects)
            - Funding & Budget (Full)
            - Publications & Outputs (All)
            - Reports (All)
            - Archive (Full)

        Director melihat SEMUA.

    2. Committee Member
    =========================================
        Fokus: governance & approval

        Menu:
            - Dashboard
            - Governance
            - Research Pipeline
                - Proposals
                - Proposal Reviews
            - Funding & Budget (View + Approval)
            - Publications (Review approval if needed)
            - Reports (View only)

        Tidak boleh edit experiment log.
        Tidak boleh edit project operasional.

    3. Reviewer
    =========================================
        Fokus: review proposal

        Menu:
            - Dashboard (My Reviews)
            - Research Pipeline
            - Assigned Proposals
            - Review Submission
            - Archive (View past review history)

        Tidak lihat:
            - Budget internal
            - Governance structure
            - Funding source detail

    4. Principal Investigator (PI)
    =========================================
        Fokus: memimpin project

        Menu:
            - Dashboard (My Projects)
            - Research Pipeline
                - My Proposals
            - Active Projects
                - My Projects
                - Milestones
                - Experiment Logs
                - Team
                - Documents
            - Funding & Budget
                - Budget Plan (Project scoped)
                - Expense Record (Project scoped)
            - Publications & Outputs (My Project)
            - Archive (My Projects)

    5. Researcher
    =========================================
        Fokus: eksekusi penelitian

        Menu:
            - Dashboard (My Assigned Tasks)
            - Active Projects
                - Assigned Projects
                - Experiment Logs
                - Milestones
                - Documents
            - Publications (Contribute only)

        Tidak bisa:
            - Approve
            - Manage budget
            - Create funding source

    6. Research Assistant
    =========================================
        Fokus: support administratif & teknis

        Menu:
            - Dashboard
            - Active Projects
                - Documents
                - Experiment Logs (limited)
            - Archive (View only)

        Biasanya lebih terbatas dari Researcher.

    7. Finance Officer
    =========================================
        Fokus: kontrol anggaran

        Menu:
            - Dashboard (Budget Overview)
            - Funding & Budget
                - Funding Sources
                - Grants
                - Budget Allocation
                - Expense Records
                - Budget Realization
            - Reports
                - Budget Report
            - Active Projects (View financial summary only)

        Tidak bisa:
            - Edit experiment log
            - Manage governance
            - Review proposal ilmiah


    8. Admin (System Operational)
    =========================================
        Fokus: operasional sistem

        Menu:
            - Dashboard
            - Governance (manage structure only)
            - Research Pipeline (operational data)
            - Active Projects (operational support)
            - Archive
            - Reports (operational)

        Tidak boleh:
            - Approve ilmiah
            - Approve funding final
            - Mengubah kebijakan
