🧠 POSISI ARSITEKTUR
=========================================
    Vertical: verticals.research

    Fokus:
        Mengelola siklus hidup penelitian dari ide → proposal → approval → eksekusi → publikasi → arsip.

    Bukan:
        Engine statistik
        AI training
        Data science platform
        Lab automation


🏛 STRUKTUR KONSEPTUAL RMS
=========================================
Kita bagi dalam 6 Domain Utama:

1️⃣ Research Governance
2️⃣ Research Lifecycle
3️⃣ Team & Role Structure
4️⃣ Funding & Budget
5️⃣ Publication & Output
6️⃣ Document & Knowledge Repository

=========================================


1. RESEARCH GOVERNANCE
=========================================
    Tujuan: mengatur struktur kelembagaan penelitian

    Fitur:
        - Research Division / Department
        - Research Program (misal: AI, Biotek, Energi)
        - Research Policy (dokumen internal)
        - Reviewer Pool
        - Ethics Committee
        - IP Committee

    Ini bukan sekadar data, tapi foundation kontrol organisasi.


2. RESEARCH LIFECYCLE (CORE ENGINE)
=========================================
    Ini jantung vertical.

    Entity Utama:
        📌 Research Idea
            - Judul
            - Deskripsi singkat
            - Pengusul
            - Bidang riset
            - Status

        📌 Research Proposal
            - Detail metodologi
            - Timeline
            - Estimasi biaya
            - Team
            - Lampiran

        📌 Proposal Review
            - Reviewer
            - Catatan revisi
            - Approval / Reject

        📌 Research Project
            Terbentuk setelah proposal approved.

            - Start date
            - End date
            - Milestones
            - Budget allocated
            - Status

        📌 Experiment Log
            - Catatan eksperimen
            - Hasil sementara
            - File pendukung
            - Timestamp

        📌 Milestone Tracking
            - Target
            - Progress
            - Delay indicator

        📌 Final Report
            - Ringkasan hasil
            - Output
            - Rekomendasi
            - Arsip


3. TEAM & ROLE STRUCTURE
=========================================
    Dalam research:
        - Principal Investigator (PI)
        - Co-Researcher
        - Research Assistant
        - Reviewer
        - Committee
        - Director of Research
        - Admin

    Ini cocok dengan Atzee role engine.

    Permission bisa granular:
        - Create proposal
        - Review proposal
        - Approve funding
        - Access experiment log
        - Publish output


4. FUNDING & BUDGET MANAGEMENT
=========================================
    Karena ini lembaga, pasti ada dana.

    Entity:
        - Funding Source
        - Grant
        - Budget Plan
        - Budget Allocation
        - Expense Record
        - Budget Realization
        - Budget Report

    Bisa integrasi dengan Accounting vertical (kalau dipakai).
    Ini powerful.


5. PUBLICATION & OUTPUT MANAGEMENT
=========================================
    Output penelitian bisa:
        - Journal Article
        - Conference Paper
        - Patent Draft
        - Prototype
        - Software
        - Dataset
        - Book Chapter

    Fitur:
        - Output registry
        - DOI reference
        - Publication status
        - Citation tracking (optional)
        - IP ownership tracking


6. DOCUMENT & KNOWLEDGE REPOSITORY
=========================================
    Research sangat document-heavy.

    Fitur:
        - Versioned document
        - Research archive
        - Tagging system
        - Search
        - Access restriction
        - Knowledge library

    Ini bisa memanfaatkan FileBlock dan TagBlock milik core UI.


🔄 WORKFLOW UTAMA (HIGH LEVEL)
=========================================
    Idea
    ↓
    Proposal Draft
    ↓
    Submit
    ↓
    Reviewer Evaluation
    ↓
    Revision Loop (optional)
    ↓
    Approval
    ↓
    Project Activation
    ↓
    Milestone Execution
    ↓
    Experiment Logs
    ↓
    Final Report
    ↓
    Publication / Patent
    ↓
    Archive

    Semua itu bisa diimplementasikan dengan WorkflowBlock.
