🧱 1. Master Data (Fondasi)
=====================================================
    Modul ini jadi referensi semua transaksi.

    + Chart of Accounts (COA)
    + Customer
    + Vendor / Supplier
    + Bank & Cash Accounts
    + Tax / Pajak
    + Currency (multi-currency)
    + Cost Center / Department


💰 2. General Ledger (Inti Accounting Engine)
=====================================================
    Jantung sistem.

    + Journal Entry (Manual)
    + Auto Journal Engine (dari modul lain)
    + Ledger (Buku Besar)
    + Trial Balance
    + Period Closing


🧾 3. Accounts Receivable (Piutang)
=====================================================
    Mengelola uang masuk dari customer.

    + Sales Invoice
    + Customer Payment
    + Aging Receivable
    + Credit Limit Management


🧾 4. Accounts Payable (Hutang)
=====================================================
    Mengelola kewajiban ke vendor.

    + Purchase Invoice
    + Vendor Payment
    + Aging Payable
    + Bill Tracking


🏦 5. Cash & Bank Management
=====================================================
    Semua pergerakan uang real.

    + Cash In / Cash Out
    + Bank Transfer
    + Bank Reconciliation
    + Multi-account tracking


📊 6. Financial Reports
=====================================================
    Output utama sistem.

    + Balance Sheet
    + Profit & Loss
    + Cash Flow
    + General Ledger Report
    + Custom Report Builder (optional tapi penting)


🧮 7. Tax Management
=====================================================
    Kalau target Indonesia → ini krusial.

    + PPN (VAT)
    + PPh
    + Tax mapping ke transaksi
    + Tax report


🔄 8. Budgeting & Control
=====================================================
    Untuk kontrol bisnis (enterprise feel).

    + Budget Planning
    + Budget vs Actual
    + Alerts / Over budget control


🔗 9. Integration Layer (Bridge ke modul lain)
=====================================================
    Karena Atzee = multi-app ERP.

    + Integrasi ke Sales
    + Integrasi ke Purchase
    + Integrasi ke Inventory
    + Integrasi ke Payroll

    (Ini bukan UI-heavy, tapi penting di architecture)


⚙️ 10. Accounting Settings & Configuration
=====================================================
    Agar fleksibel (white-label ready).

    + Fiscal Year
    + Accounting Method (Cash / Accrual)
    + Auto numbering
    + Default accounts mapping
    + Multi-company config


🧠 11. Audit & Compliance
=====================================================
    Untuk enterprise credibility.

    + Audit Trail (siapa ubah apa)
    + Journal lock
    + Approval system (opsional tapi powerful)


🔐 12. Role-based Finance Access
=====================================================
    Karena kamu sudah punya sistem role global.

    + Finance Viewer
    + Accountant
    + Finance Manager
    + Auditor
