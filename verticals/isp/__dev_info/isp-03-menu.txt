📡 MASTER MENU STRUCTURE – ISP
=========================================
    Ini adalah global menu tree.
    Nanti setiap role hanya melihat subset sesuai permission.

    Dashboard

    Customer Management
        Customers
        Installations
        Subscriptions
        Packages
        Coverage Area

    Billing & Finance
        Invoices
        Payments
        Recurring Billing
        Penalties
        Financial Reports

    Network Management
        Devices
        OLT / Routers
        IP Pools
        Bandwidth Profiles
        Network Topology
        Monitoring
        Active Sessions

    Operations
        Trouble Tickets
        Work Orders
        Schedule
        SLA Monitoring

    Sales & Marketing
        Leads
        Sales Orders
        Promotions
        Commission Tracking

    Reports
        Revenue Report
        Customer Growth
        Network Usage
        Downtime Report
        Technician Performance

    System
        Users
        Roles & Permissions
        Tenant Settings
        Integration Settings

    Sekarang kita breakdown menu per role.


1. OWNER
=========================================
    Akses: SEMUA

    Menu:
        - Semua menu global
        - Full reports
        - Tenant configuration
        - Integration configuration
        - Device configuration
        - Financial reports


2. General Manager
=========================================
    Fokus ke KPI & kontrol.

    Menu:
        # Dashboard
            - KPI Overview
            - Revenue Summary
            - Active Customer
            - Network Status Summary

        # Customer Management
            - Customers
            - Subscriptions
            - Installations

        # Billing & Finance
            - Invoices (read)
            - Payments (read)
            - Financial Reports

        # Network Management
            - Monitoring (read)
            - Network Summary

        # Operations
            - Tickets
            - SLA Monitoring

        # Reports
            - Semua laporan

    Tidak bisa:
        - Edit device
        - Ubah bandwidth profile
        - Edit role system


3. Network Engineer
=========================================
    Menu:
        Network Management
            - Devices
            - OLT / Routers
            - IP Pools
            - VLAN
            - Bandwidth Profiles
            - Active Sessions
            - Network Topology
            - Monitoring

        Customer Management
            - Subscriptions (technical view)
            - PPPoE Accounts

    Tidak bisa:
        - Invoice
        - Payment
        - Financial reports


4. NOC Staff
=========================================
    Menu:
        Dashboard
            - Live Network Status
            - Active Alerts

        Network Management
            - Monitoring
            - Active Sessions
            - Restart Session
            - Temporary Suspend

        Operations
            - Trouble Tickets
            - SLA Monitoring

    Tidak bisa:
        - Device config
        - Billing
        - Paket management


5. Billing Staff
=========================================
    Menu:
        Billing & Finance
            - Invoices
            - Payments
            - Recurring Billing
            - Penalties
            - Suspend/Unsuspend (billing trigger)

        Customer Management
            - Customers (billing view)
            - Subscriptions

        Reports
            - Aging Receivable
            - Overdue List

    Tidak bisa:
        - Network device
        - IP pool
        - Monitoring


6. Customer Service
=========================================
    Menu:
        Customer Management
            - Customers
            - Subscriptions
            - Installations

        Operations
            - Trouble Tickets
            - Work Orders
            - Schedule Technician

        Billing & Finance
            - Invoice (read only)
            - Payment Status (read only)

    Tidak bisa:
        - Edit paket
        - Ubah device
        - Ubah financial


7. Field Technician
=========================================
    Menu:
        Operations
            - My Work Orders
            - Installation Tasks
            - Ticket Assignments

        Customer Management
            - Customer Detail (limited)
            - Update MAC / ONU
            - Upload Photo Evidence

    Tidak bisa:
        - Billing
        - Reports
        - Device management


8. Sales Marketing
=========================================
    Menu:
        Sales & Marketing
            - Leads
            - Sales Orders
            - Promotions
            - Commission Tracking

        Customer Management
            - Create Customer
            - Submit Installation Request

        Reports
            - Sales Performance
            - Target Tracking

    Tidak bisa:
        - Billing full access
        - Network config


9. Finance Manager
=========================================
    Menu:
        Billing & Finance
            - Invoices
            - Payments
            - Recurring Billing
            - Financial Reports
            - Cashflow
            - Write-off Approval

        Reports
            - Revenue
            - Profit
            - AR Aging
            - Customer Payment Trend

    Tidak bisa:
        - Network config


10. Customer (Portal)
=========================================
    Menu:
        Dashboard
            - Connection Status
            - Usage Summary

        My Account
            - My Subscription
            - My Package
            - Upgrade/Downgrade Request

        Billing
            - My Invoices
            - Payment History
            - Pay Now

        Support
            - Create Ticket
            - My Tickets
            - Installation Status
