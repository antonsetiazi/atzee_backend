🔐 ROLE STRUCTURE
=========================================
    - Owner
    - General Manager
    - Network Engineer
    - NOC Staff
    - Billing Staff
    - Customer Service
    - Field Technician
    - Sales Marketing
    - Finance Manager
    - Customer

Sekarang kita breakdown satu per satu supaya jelas domain-nya.


1. Owner
=========================================
    Level tertinggi tenant.

    Akses:
        - Semua laporan
        - Semua modul
        - Override suspend
        - Konfigurasi device utama
        - Melihat profit/loss

    ⚠ Tidak selalu ikut operasional harian.


2. General Manager
=========================================
    Mengelola operasional harian ISP.

    Akses:
        - Dashboard KPI
        - Monitoring pelanggan
        - Approval diskon
        - Approval paket khusus
        - Monitoring SLA


3. Network Engineer
=========================================
    Fokus ke infrastruktur & provisioning.

    Akses:
        - Device management
        - OLT / Mikrotik
        - Bandwidth profile
        - Network topology
        - IP pool
        - Radius
        - VLAN

    Tidak boleh:
        - Edit invoice
        - Edit pembayaran


4. NOC Staff (Network Operation Center)
=========================================
    Monitoring 24 jam.

    Akses:
        - Lihat status online/offline
        - Create trouble ticket
        - Restart user session
        - Suspend sementara

    Tidak boleh:
        - Edit harga paket
        - Edit accounting


5. Billing Staff
=========================================
    Fokus ke tagihan.

    Akses:
        - Generate invoice
        - Monitor overdue
        - Apply denda
        - Suspend otomatis
        - Re-activate jika bayar

    Tidak boleh:
        - Konfigurasi device
        - Ubah bandwidth


6. Customer Service
=========================================
    Frontliner.

    Akses:
        - Lihat data customer
        - Buat ticket gangguan
        - Cek status pembayaran
        - Cek status jaringan
        - Reschedule teknisi

    Tidak boleh:
        - Edit paket
        - Ubah invoice


7. Field Technician
=========================================
    Lapangan.

    Akses:
        - Lihat work order
        - Update status instalasi
        - Upload foto bukti
        - Update MAC address / ONU

    Tidak boleh:
        - Lihat laporan keuangan
        - Akses semua customer


8. Sales Marketing
=========================================
    Akses:
        - Input calon customer
        - Buat penawaran paket
        - Lihat komisi
        - Tracking target bulanan

    Tidak boleh:
        - Akses billing global
        - Akses device


9. Finance Manager
=========================================
    Level lebih tinggi dari billing staff.

    Akses:
        - Laporan revenue
        - Aging receivable
        - Cashflow
        - Approval write-off
        - Diskon massal


10. Customer (Self Portal)
=========================================
    Role khusus portal pelanggan.

    Akses:
        - Lihat tagihan
        - Bayar
        - Lihat pemakaian
        - Lihat status koneksi
        - Buat tiket gangguan

    Tidak bisa:
        - Lihat customer lain
        - Akses internal data


🎯 Struktur Hierarki Akses
=========================================
    100 - Owner
    90  - General Manager
    80  - Finance Manager
    70  - Network Engineer
    60  - NOC Staff
    50  - Billing Staff
    40  - Customer Service
    30  - Sales Marketing
    20  - Field Technician
    10  - Customer
