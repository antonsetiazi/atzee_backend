🏦 STRUKTUR MENU GLOBAL (CBS)
=========================================
    Sebelum dibagi per role, kita tentukan dulu Master Menu Domain CBS.

    CBS idealnya punya 10 domain utama:
        1. Dashboard
        2. Customer (CIF)
        3. Accounts
        4. Transactions
        5. Loans
        6. Treasury
        7. Clearing & Settlement
        8. Compliance
        9. Reports
        10. Administration

    Semua role hanya mendapatkan subset sesuai permission.


1. DIRECTOR
=========================================
    Kontrol penuh tenant level

    Menu
        + Dashboard
            - Bank Performance
            - Liquidity Overview
            - Loan Portfolio Summary
            - Risk Exposure
            - NPL Ratio

        + Customer
            - All Customers
            - Risk Segmentation
            - High Risk Customers

        + Accounts
            - All Accounts
            - Dormant Accounts
            - Large Balance Accounts

        + Transactions
            - Daily Transaction Summary
            - Suspicious Transactions

        + Loans
            - Loan Portfolio
            - NPL Monitoring
            - Restructured Loans

        + Treasury
            - Liquidity Ratio
            - Interbranch Position

        + Compliance
            - AML Dashboard
            - Regulatory Reports
            - Audit Trail

        + Reports
            - Financial Statement
            - Branch Performance
            - Profitability Analysis

        + Administration
            - Branch Management
            - Product Management
            - Interest Configuration
            - Limit Configuration
