1. Struktur Utama Website (Site Architecture)
=========================================
    Struktur yang sederhana tapi sangat kuat untuk personal brand global:

    antonsetiazi.com
        │
        ├── Home
        ├── About
        ├── Services
        │     ├── ERP Development
        │     ├── SaaS Platform Development
        │     ├── Backend Architecture
        │     └── Technical Consulting
        │
        ├── Work / Portfolio
        │
        ├── Insights / Blog
        │
        └── Contact

    Prinsipnya:
        - tidak terlalu banyak halaman
        - jelas dan profesional
        - fokus pada problem bisnis


2. Homepage Structure (Halaman Paling Penting)
=========================================
    Homepage adalah landing page utama untuk klien global.

    Struktur ideal:
        - Hero
        - Trust Section
        - Services
        - Expertise
        - Featured Work
        - Insights
        - Call To Action
        - Footer


3. Hero Section (Above the Fold)
=========================================

15. Struktur Folder Website (Untuk Dev)
=========================================
    Karena kamu developer, saya sarankan struktur seperti ini:

    src
    │
    ├── components
    │
    ├── sections
    │     ├── hero
    │     ├── services
    │     ├── expertise
    │     ├── portfolio
    │     ├── blog
    │
    ├── pages
    │     ├── home
    │     ├── about
    │     ├── services
    │     ├── portfolio
    │     ├── blog
    │     └── contact
    │
    └── data
        services.json
        portfolio.json
        blog.json

    Ini cocok dengan sistem landing architecture yang kamu bangun sebelumnya.
