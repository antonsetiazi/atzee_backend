Konsep Vertical Marketplace untuk Atzee
=========================================
    Vertical ini adalah platform yang mempertemukan seller dan buyer.

    Contoh produk:
        - Tokopedia
        - Shopee
        - Lazada
        - Blibli
        - Bukalapak
        - Amazon
        - Etsy


Posisi Marketplace dalam Arsitektur Atzee
=========================================
    Vertical ini akan menggunakan Business Layer yang sudah ada.

    Misalnya:

    CORE
    ├── auth
    ├── tenant
    ├── role
    ├── permission
    └── ui schema

    BUSINESS
    ├── product
    ├── order
    ├── payment
    ├── customer
    ├── inventory
    └── finance

    VERTICALS
    ├── pos
    ├── koperasi
    ├── isp
    ├── banking
    └── marketplace   ← baru

    Marketplace tidak membuat ulang sistem order.

    Marketplace hanya menambah:
        - seller
        - commission
        - escrow
        - shipment
        - review
        - store


Konsep Multi-Vendor Marketplace
=========================================
    Marketplace memiliki 3 aktor utama:

    | Actor  | Peran                 |
    | ------ | --------------------- |
    | Buyer  | Pembeli               |
    | Seller | Penjual               |
    | Admin  | Pengelola marketplace |



Modul Utama Marketplace
=========================================
    Berikut modul yang realistis untuk marketplace modern.

    1. Store / Seller Management
    -------------------------------------------
        Mengelola penjual di marketplace.

        + Fitur:
            - Seller registration
            - Store profile
            - Store verification
            - Store rating
            - Store status
            - Store policy

        + Entity:
            - Store
            - SellerProfile
            - StoreVerification
            - StorePolicy
            - StoreRating

    2. Product Marketplace
    -------------------------------------------
        Produk milik seller.

        + Fitur:
            - Seller product listing
            - Product variant
            - Product category
            - Product image
            - Product approval
            - Product moderation

        + Entity:
            - MarketplaceProduct
            - ProductVariant
            - ProductCategory
            - ProductImage
            - ProductModeration

    3. Catalog & Discovery
    -------------------------------------------
        Agar pembeli bisa menemukan produk.

        + Fitur:
            - Search
            - Filter
            - Category
            - Recommendation
            - Trending products
            - Flash sale

        + Entity:
            - CatalogIndex
            - SearchQuery
            - ProductRecommendation
            - FlashSale

    4. Cart & Checkout
    -------------------------------------------
        Keranjang belanja.

        + Fitur:
            - Multi store cart
            - Voucher
            - Discount
            - Shipping selection
            - Checkout

        + Entity:
            - Cart
            - CartItem
            - Checkout
            - CheckoutSession
    
    5. Order System
    -------------------------------------------
        Order marketplace lebih kompleks dari POS.

        + Karena:
            1 order bisa berisi banyak seller.

            Contoh:
                Order
                ├─ OrderItem (seller A)
                ├─ OrderItem (seller B)

        + Entity:
            - MarketplaceOrder
            - MarketplaceOrderItem
            - OrderSplit

    6. Payment System
    -------------------------------------------
        Pembayaran marketplace biasanya:

        Escrow (ditahan dulu)

        + Alur:
            Buyer bayar
            ↓
            Marketplace tahan uang
            ↓
            Seller kirim barang
            ↓
            Buyer konfirmasi
            ↓
            Marketplace release dana

        + Entity:
            - Payment
            - Escrow
            - Payout
            - Commission

    7. Shipping / Logistics
    -------------------------------------------
        + Fitur:
            - Shipping calculation
            - Courier integration
            - Shipment tracking
            - Delivery status

        + Entity:
            - Shipment
            - ShipmentItem
            - ShipmentTracking
            - CourierService

    8. Review & Rating
    -------------------------------------------
        + Fitur:
            - Product review
            - Store review
            - Photo review
            - Report review

        + Entity:
            - Review
            - ReviewPhoto
            - ReviewVote
            - ReviewReport

    9. Promotion System
    -------------------------------------------
        Fitur marketing.

        + Contoh:
            - Voucher
            - Cashback
            - Flash sale
            - Campaign
            - Affiliate

        + Entity:
            - Voucher
            - Campaign
            - FlashSale
            - Affiliate

    10. Seller Finance
    -------------------------------------------
        Menghitung uang seller.

        + Fitur:
            - Sales report
            - Commission
            - Seller balance
            - Withdrawal

        + Entity:
            - SellerBalance
            - SellerTransaction
            - SellerPayout
            - CommissionRule

Kenapa Marketplace Cocok untuk Atzee
=========================================
    Karena marketplace adalah vertical yang sangat kompleks.

    Jika berhasil dibuat generik:
        Atzee bisa membuat produk seperti:
            - Shopee clone
            - Tokopedia clone
            - B2B marketplace
            - wholesale marketplace
            - service marketplace
            - digital goods marketplace

    Semua hanya dengan mengaktifkan vertical.


Potensi SaaS Besar
=========================================
    Jika vertical ini selesai:

    Atzee bisa membuat SaaS:
        - Marketplace UMKM
        - Marketplace desa
        - Marketplace koperasi
        - Marketplace sekolah
        - Marketplace kampus
        - Marketplace komunitas
